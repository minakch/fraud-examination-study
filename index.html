Benford's Law First-Digit Distribution Analyzer

According to Benford's Law, in naturally occurring financial transactions, the digit 1 appears as the leading first digit ~30.1% of the time, while 9 appears only ~4.6% of the time. When perpetrators fabricate numbers or split checks below managerial approval thresholds, the distribution spikes dramatically.
